# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based dashboard designed to analyze e-commerce sales, customer behavior, product performance, payment methods, sales channels, brands, cities, and regions.

The dashboard transforms raw e-commerce data into meaningful visual insights using **Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPIs, and Slicers**, helping users quickly understand business performance and identify trends.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance
- Track total sales, orders, customers, quantity sold, and profit
- Identify top and bottom-performing products
- Analyze sales performance by brand
- Understand customer purchasing behavior
- Compare sales across different cities and regions
- Analyze different payment methods
- Compare sales across different sales channels
- Monitor monthly sales and profit trends
- Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

---

## 📌 Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard provides key performance indicators such as:

- 💰 **Total Sales**
- 🛍️ **Total Orders**
- 👥 **Total Customers**
- 📦 **Total Quantity Sold**
- 💵 **Total Profit**
- 🧾 **Average Order Value (AOV)**

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the overall business performance.

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Monthly Sales & Profit

The monthly trend chart shows how **sales and profit change throughout the year**.

It helps identify:
- High-performing months
- Low-performing months
- Sales trends
- Profit trends
- Changes in business performance over time

### 2. Sales & Profit by Category

This section compares sales and profit across different product categories.

It helps determine:
- Which category generates the highest sales
- Which category generates the highest profit
- Which categories require improvement

### 3. Top 10 Products

The **Top 10 Products** chart identifies products that contribute the most to sales.

This helps businesses understand their best-selling products and focus on high-performing products.

### 4. Bottom 10 Products

The **Bottom 10 Products** chart highlights products with relatively lower sales performance.

This can help businesses:
- Review product demand
- Improve marketing strategies
- Identify products that may need attention
- Optimize product inventory

### 5. Sales by City

The city-wise analysis compares sales performance across different cities.

It helps identify:
- Highest-performing cities
- Lowest-performing cities
- Geographic sales opportunities

### 6. Sales by Region

The dashboard compares sales and profit across regions such as:

- North
- South
- West

This provides a clear understanding of regional business performance.

### 7. Top 10 Customers by Sales

The **Top 10 Customer by Sale** analysis identifies customers who contribute the highest sales.

This can support:
- Customer retention strategies
- Customer segmentation
- Loyalty programs
- Targeted marketing

### 8. Sales by Brand

The brand analysis compares sales across different brands, helping identify the strongest and weakest-performing brands.

### 9. Sales by Payment Method

The dashboard analyzes sales through different payment methods:

- COD
- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- Net Banking
- UPI

This helps understand customer payment preferences.

### 10. Sales by Sales Channel

Sales are also analyzed across different channels:

- Marketplace
- Mobile App
- Website

This helps identify which sales channel generates the most revenue.
